## 问题3：更精确的英文解释（替代/补充命令#25的"reduced statistical power"表述）

两点均成立，可以给出比"样本量不足"更精确的机制性解释：

> *"The weakened quadratic term in the weather_natural-only exposure model is not merely a consequence of reduced sample size. Cause Code attribution to weather_natural rises near-monotonically with gust intensity (from 6.9% in the lowest gust decile to 56.1% in the highest), meaning the classification process itself is not independent of the wind level it is meant to explain. As a direct consequence, the weather_natural subsample is disproportionately depleted of low-gust anchor points — only 36.8% of its observations fall below the estimated turning point, versus 63.7% in the full main-specification sample — precisely the region needed to pin down the descending arm of the U-shaped gust–exposure curve. This selection-induced range restriction on the low-gust side, rather than sample size alone, plausibly explains why the quadratic term becomes statistically unreliable in this subsample while the recovery-side quadratic term (which does not rely as heavily on resolving a low-gust anchor, per command #25) replicates cleanly."*

（中文对应表述：weather_natural子样本E0二次项变弱，不能只归因于样本量缩小。Cause Code被判定为weather_natural的概率本身随阵风强度近乎单调上升——这意味着分类过程本身不独立于它试图解释的阵风水平。直接后果是weather_natural子样本在低阵风端的锚点被系统性稀释——只有36.8%的观测点落在拐点以下，而全量主规格样本这一比例是63.7%——恰恰是锚定U型曲线下降段所必需的区间。这种由选择效应导致的低阵风端变异范围受限，比单纯的样本量不足更精确地解释了为什么二次项在这个子样本里变得不可靠。）
