## 论文标题与摘要

**标题**：*Weather-Related Power Outage Impacts in UK Electricity Distribution Systems: Gust Responses, Environmental Modulation, and Regional Heterogeneity*

**核心摘要要点**：基于UKPN超过44,000条天气相关停电事件，分别对暴露（受影响客户数）和恢复（恢复时长）建模，发现阵风-停电关系呈非线性（阵风越强，影响上升越陡）；停电响应随周边天气条件和区域而变化；稳健性检验显示核心非线性关系基本稳定，但部分暴露侧交互效应对更保守的聚类假设敏感。

## LAD风敏感性地图的精确定位

**对应正文 Figure 6**（第4.3节"Spatial heterogeneity in model-based conditional outage estimates"），标题为：

> *Spatial heterogeneity in model-based predicted outage impacts under a common reference weather scenario: (a) Predicted affected customers; (b) Predicted outage duration.*

配图说明（图注）：

> *Predictions are generated by holding weather conditions constant across LADs while allowing observed LAD-level regional covariates to vary. Exposure predictions are expressed in absolute affected customers and therefore partly reflect differences in customer exposure across LADs. Additional estimates for regional contextual covariates are reported in Appendix E1.*
