## 草稿一：引言部分，用于引出研究动机（仿Mukherjee et al. 2018的具体案例开篇方式）

> In February 2022, three named storms — Dudley, Eunice, and Franklin — struck the United Kingdom within five days. Within UK Power Networks' service area alone, Storm Eunice on 18 February produced a peak observed gust of 39.4 m/s (approximately 88.2 mph) in Lewes, East Sussex — the highest gust recorded across the entire 2021–2024 study period — and was associated with 1,601 weather-related outage events across 101 Local Authority Districts, cumulatively affecting an estimated 376,712 customers, with a mean restoration time of 52.7 hours. Two days later, Storm Franklin affected a further 1,187 events, with the Wealden district — already the most heavily affected area during Eunice — again recording the highest cumulative customer impact (40,067 affected customers). These compounding events illustrate both the scale of weather-related disruption electricity distribution networks can face and the operational relevance of understanding how outage impacts respond to escalating wind conditions.

（数字来源：Step1核实表——Eunice n=1,601/gust 39.40 m/s/customers_v2总和376,712/duration_B均值52.72h/101个LAD；Step2案例二——Franklin Wealden customers_v2=40,067。**明确区分**：39.4 m/s是UKPN区域内观测值，不是怀特岛122mph的全国性纪录，两者不可混淆——正文如引用122mph，需单独注明来源是气象局全国记录、非本数据集。）

## 草稿二：结果讨论部分，用于印证临界风速阈值的实际意义

> The estimated critical wind speed of 10.69 m/s identified in the exposure model (Section X) is not an abstract statistical construct: it is exceeded by a wide margin during every one of the seven major named storms examined in this study (Storm Arwen, Dudley, Eunice, Franklin, Babet, Ciarán, and Henk), with observed peak gusts within the UKPN service area ranging from 22.0 m/s (Storm Arwen, 2.06 times the threshold) to 39.4 m/s (Storm Eunice, 3.69 times the threshold). During Storm Eunice, 96.1% of the 1,601 associated outage events (1,538 events) occurred under gust conditions above this threshold, placing the overwhelming majority of storm-related disruption within the accelerating-response regime identified by the model.

（数字来源：Step1核实表全部7场风暴的gust_max值及与10.69m/s的倍数关系；Step2案例一——Eunice 1,538/1,601=96.1%超过阈值。）

## 草稿三：讨论部分，用于说明恢复时长的极端拖尾现象（呼应方差分解"权重有限"论点的定性佐证）

> Even among the most severe storms in the dataset, restoration times were highly heterogeneous. During Storm Franklin, the mean restoration duration was 47.4 hours, yet the longest individual case reached 1,728.3 hours (approximately 72 days) — the longest single restoration recorded among the seven storms examined. Such extreme tail cases, alongside the comparatively modest peak gust observed during the flood-dominated Storm Babet (22.8 m/s, the second-lowest among the seven storms) which nonetheless produced the longest maximum restoration time overall (1,890.5 hours), illustrate why gust intensity alone explains only a limited share of the variation in outage recovery outcomes — consistent with the variance decomposition results in Section X, where affected-customer scale accounts for substantially more explanatory power than wind conditions.

（数字来源：Step2案例二——Franklin均值47.36h/最长1,728.30h；Step1核实表——Babet gust_max 22.80 m/s（7场中第二低）/duration_B_max 1,890.47h（7场中最长）。**注意**：草稿三引用的两个"最长duration"数字均为风暴窗口内未经p99截尾的原始最大值，引用时应保留Step2已加的提醒——这些是记录到的极端个案，不代表模型拟合时使用的典型数值范围，若在论文正文引用，建议加脚注说明。）

## 使用提醒

1. 三段草稿的全部数字均可逐一追溯到`10_命名风暴核实.md`（Step1）或`11_重点案例详细数据.md`（Step2）的具体表格，没有任何未经核实的数字。
2. 草稿一和草稿二里的"39.4 m/s"必须与"122mph全国纪录"分开引用，不能在同一句话里混用两个不同区域尺度的数字而不加说明。
3. 草稿三引用的两个极端最长恢复时长（1,728.3h、1,890.5h）建议在正文脚注中说明"未经p99截尾处理的窗口内原始最大值"，避免读者将其误认为模型样本内的代表性数值。
4. 三段草稿均为英文（与论文正文语言一致），如需要可另行翻译为中文供网页端内部讨论使用。
